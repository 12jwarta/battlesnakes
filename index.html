Rules

Welcome to Battlesnakes! You are the Green Snake (Left). You battle the Blue Snake (Right). You win by doing one of the following:

  • Getting to 15 points first.
  • Reducing your opponent to zero health.
  • Having your opponent receive damage three game ticks in a row.

Food Blocks

You gain and lose points with the following food blocks:

(Left) (Right)
Point food: +1 point. Respawn all blocks Point food: +1 point. Respawn all blocks
+1 point if your points are below your body length. You lose one point (minimum 0).
Enemy loses one point (minimum 0). +1 point if enemy points are below its body length.

Strategy note: when a snake collects a point block, the next point block spawns on that snake's opponent side of the board, regardless of where the point was collected. If your opponent starts scoring in your territory, point spawns can snowball against you. Look for chances to take point blocks in your opponent's territory whenever possible.

Collision and Damage

A snake takes damage if its head collides with a wall, its own body segment, or its opponents body segment. Both snakes will be damaged in the event of a head-on collision. Upon taking damage, a snake will freeze in place and flash temporarily. If possible, a damaged snake will move one tile in a random direction.

Controls: Arrow keys or WASD to move, Space to pause, R to restart.
