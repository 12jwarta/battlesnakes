Rules

Initialization

Board is 17x17. Starting point food appears one tile left or right of center. Starting points and starting size for each snake come from Settings, then the snake farther from that starting food gets +1 body segment. Starting body segments begin stacked at each head and spread out as the snake moves.

Food Blocks

Red block: Point food. Collecting it gives +1 point and starts a one-tick delay, then all food types respawn together.

Light green block: Player food. If player collects it and player points are below player body length, player gains +1 point. If enemy collects it, player loses 1 point (minimum 0).

Light blue block: Enemy food. If enemy collects it and enemy points are below enemy body length, enemy gains +1 point. If player collects it, enemy loses 1 point (minimum 0).

Snake Blocks

Dark head block: Snake head.

Body block: Snake body segments.

Flashing snake: Stunned from damage and temporarily not moving.

Damage and Losing

On collision damage, a snake loses 1 body segment and 1 point (points do not go below 0), then gets a brief stun. If possible, damaged snakes separate by one tile in random directions. A snake loses by taking damage three times in a row, or if damage drops its body below one segment.

Winning

First snake to 15 points wins.

Controls: Arrow keys or WASD to move, Space to pause, R to restart.
